Data Deletion and Access Revocation

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Cyber Alert News is an administrator-only publishing service. It does not collect personal information from LinkedIn Page followers, readers, or other members of the public.

Revoke LinkedIn access

The authorized administrator can revoke the service's access from LinkedIn:

  1. Sign in to LinkedIn.
  2. Open Settings & Privacy.
  3. Open the section that lists permitted services or connected applications.
  4. Locate the Cyber Alert News application and remove its access.

LinkedIn may change the names or location of these settings. Revoking access prevents the service from making future LinkedIn API requests with that authorization.

Request deletion

To request deletion of information associated with the integration, email cyberalertnepal@proton.me with the subject “Cyber Alert News data deletion request.”

Please include enough information to identify the relevant administrator account or LinkedIn Page. Do not send passwords or access tokens by email. Requests will be acknowledged and handled within 30 days, subject to any information that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

What will be deleted

Where applicable, deletion includes stored LinkedIn identifiers, authorization credentials, and technical records associated with the integration. Published LinkedIn posts may need to be deleted separately from the LinkedIn Page.
