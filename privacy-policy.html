Cyber Alert News / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Cyber Alert News is an administrator-operated service that publishes global cybersecurity alerts to the Cyber-Alert-News LinkedIn Page. It is not available for public registration or use.

Information processed

Cyber Alert News does not collect personal information from LinkedIn Page followers, readers, or members of the public. It does not access their profiles, contacts, messages, comments, or reactions.

To publish to the Page, the service may process limited information associated with its authorized administrator and LinkedIn integration:

  • LinkedIn authorization credentials, such as an OAuth access token;
  • the LinkedIn Page or organization identifier;
  • the administrator or account identifier and Page role when required for authorization;
  • content selected for publication, source URLs, publication timestamps, and technical error logs.

How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate the authorized administrator, verify permission to manage the Page, publish cybersecurity alerts, maintain service security, and diagnose failed publication attempts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions about individuals.

Storage and retention

Authorization credentials are restricted to the administrator and the systems needed to operate the service. They are retained only while required for the LinkedIn integration and are removed or invalidated when access is revoked or the service is discontinued. Technical logs are retained only for operational and security purposes and are deleted when no longer needed.

Sharing and service providers

Cyber Alert News does not sell or rent personal information. Limited data may be processed by LinkedIn to authorize and publish posts and by infrastructure providers used to operate the service. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set its own cookies and does not use advertising or analytics tools.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect authorization credentials and limit access to the administrator. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and rights

The authorized administrator may revoke the application's LinkedIn access at any time. Anyone may contact Cyber Alert News to ask whether information about them is held or to request access, correction, or deletion. Because the service does not collect information from Page followers or readers, it will ordinarily hold no information about them.

See the Data Deletion and Access Revocation page for instructions.

Third-party content and services

Posts may link to reporting published by third parties such as Heise Online. Cyber Alert News is independent of and is not endorsed by Heise Online or LinkedIn. Following an external link is subject to that provider's own terms and privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The latest version and effective date will remain available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email cyberalertnepal@proton.me.
